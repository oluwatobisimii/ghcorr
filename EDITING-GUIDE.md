# Updating the GHC Outer Ring Road website

You don't need to touch any code. Everything is done in the website dashboard.

**Dashboard:** https://app.pagescms.org (sign in with the email you were invited with)

Every change you save goes live on its own in about two minutes. Every change is also kept in the site's history, so mistakes can be undone.

---

## Post a sermon (every Sunday)

1. Open **Sermons** and click **Add an entry**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title:** the message title, e.g. *The God of Daniel*
   - **Date preached**
   - **Preacher:** e.g. *Pastor Abimbola Richards*
   - **Series:** only if it's part of a series. Use exactly the same spelling every week so the messages group together.
   - **Bible passage:** e.g. *Daniel 6:26*
   - **Big idea:** one or two sentences
   - **YouTube link:** paste the link from YouTube. The picture and video player are added automatically.
   - **Sermon notes** (optional): key points, scriptures and quotes
3. Click **Save**.

The newest sermon appears on the homepage automatically. Don't have the YouTube link yet? Upload a **Photo** instead, and add the link later.

## Add an event or programme

1. Open **Events & programmes** and click **Add an entry**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title**
   - **Starts:** the date and time it starts
   - **Ends:** when it's over (leave it blank for a one-day event)
   - **Date text people see:** e.g. *Sat 5 Dec · 9:00am*
   - **Venue / details line**
   - **Banner:** export it at **2400 × 1000 px** as a JPG under 600 KB
   - **Button text** and **Button link** (optional), e.g. a Google Form for registration
3. Click **Save**.

The event shows in the homepage slider and on the **Events** page. **Once the end date passes, it moves to Past events by itself.** Nobody needs to remember to take it down.

Turn on **Pin as first slide on the homepage** for the biggest upcoming event.

## Big events with their own page (like the anniversary)

Turn on **Give this event its own page**, then fill in the **Page:** sections you need. Skip any section you don't need. The page lives at `ghcouterringroad.org/events/<file name>/`.

| Section | What it's for |
|---|---|
| Page: top section | Big title, video or picture, countdown and buttons. Tick **Hide after the event** on buttons like "Buy T-shirt". |
| Page: the word | A quote. Put words in `*stars*` to make them orange. |
| Page: schedule | One card per day. |
| Page: ministers | Photos and short bios. |
| Page: get involved | T-shirt, giving, sponsorship cards. These hide automatically after the event. |
| Page: posters & videos | Downloadable posters. For daily countdown posters, upload 1.jpg … 13.jpg and today.jpg into one folder. |
| Page: recap | **Fill this in after the event:** the YouTube link of the service and the best photos. It appears by itself once the event is over. |
| DP maker | Personalised DP page. Ask the media lead for the photo window numbers. |

## Weekly programmes

**Weekly programmes** are the regular services: Celebration Service, Teaching & Communion and Morning Prayers. They always show in the slider after the dated events.

## Site settings

**Site settings** holds the phone number, email, social links, service times (used for the "next service" countdown on the homepage) and the giving link.

**Highlighted menu item** is the orange link in the top menu (currently "15th Anniversary"). It disappears on its own after that event, so set a new one for the next big event.

## Picture sizes

| What | Size |
|---|---|
| Programme or event banner | 2400 × 1000 px, JPG |
| Sermon photo | 1600 × 900 px, JPG |
| Minister photo | 1000 × 1000 px or larger |
| DP design | 1080 × 1350 px |

Keep photos under about **600 KB**. Export them as JPG at 80% quality.

## If something looks wrong

- **The change isn't showing:** wait two minutes and refresh. On phones, pull down to refresh.
- **"Build failed":** a field is probably in the wrong format, usually a date. Open the last thing you edited, fix it and save again. The live site keeps the last good version until then.
- **Still stuck:** contact the web admin. Every change is in the history and can be rolled back.
