## Key points

1. **Remember whose you are.** We are Christ's disciples, so our attitude and character must reflect Him, even when we are not preaching.
2. **Let your light shine.** "Let" means allow. The light is already in us, and it is our job to let it shine (Matthew 5:16).
3. **Work out, don't work for.** Salvation is a free gift, but we must work out what God has worked in (Philippians 2:12).
4. **Discipline the body.** Being born again inside doesn't make everything automatic. The flesh wars against the spirit, so it takes effort.

> "Christian conduct is the outward evidence of an inward transformation."
